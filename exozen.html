01 / The science

Growth factor concentrate with exosome-associated signalling.

Exozen is an autologous regenerative protocol combining Growth Factor Concentrate with exosome-associated signalling for skin rejuvenation, scar repair and hair restoration.

Prepared from the patient’s own blood, Exozen is designed to support biocompatibility and patient-specific treatment planning.

What Exozen contains

Autologous Exosomes are nanosized vesicles enriched with mRNA, proteins and lipids that communicate regenerative signals between cells. Growth Factor Concentrate (GFC) contains platelet-derived growth factors including PDGF, VEGF, EGF, TGF-β and bFGF to support angiogenesis, collagen synthesis and tissue regeneration.
