PRP vs GFC vs EXOSGFC COMPARISION

Compare what each preparation contains and how it is processed.

PRP

Platelet-rich plasma

What it provides
A plasma preparation enriched with platelets and containing plasma-derived bioactive factors.
Key component
Platelets.
Process
Centrifugation concentrates platelets from the patient's blood.

GFC

Growth factor concentrate

What it provides
A plasma fraction that concentrates growth factors released by activated platelets.
Key component
Concentrated growth factors.
Process
Platelet activation followed by controlled centrifugation yields a growth factor-rich fraction.

EXOSGFC

Exosome-rich plasma

What it provides
Growth factor-rich plasma containing platelet-derived extracellular vesicles, including vesicles in the exosome size range.
Key component
Exosome-sized vesicles and growth factors.
Process
Controlled platelet activation, differential centrifugation and bi-phase filtration. The illustrated process uses a 0.2 µm filtration stage.

EXOSGFC system information

System Features

EXOSGFC kits are designed for preparation of an autologous blood component in a clinical setting.

Closed system
The system limits environmental exposure during preparation, from collection through transfer of the concentrate.
Blood-contact components
Components intended to contact blood are supplied sterile and nonpyrogenic.
Single-patient use
Use each kit for one patient only and discard it after use.
Sterilization and quality controls
Gamma irradiation is used for terminal sterilization. Check the kit documentation for sterilization and quality system details.
Safety & Regulatory Information

Rx only. Use requires physician oversight. The kits are supplied through licensed medical professionals and are unsuitable for self-administration.

Patient populations
Use is not established for patients under 18, pregnant or breastfeeding patients, people with an active systemic infection, or people with blood-borne pathogen exposure.
Blood collection
Use an evacuated tube collection system as specified in the kit instructions. Alternative collection methods can compromise sample quality.
Disposal after use
Do not reuse, reprocess or resterilize any component. Reuse can transmit infection or lead to device failure.
Sterile packaging
Inspect seals before opening. Set aside any kit with packaging that is already open, damaged or otherwise compromised.
Storage & Handling

For Osteogen and Exozen, follow the temperature range and handling conditions printed on the specific kit.

Temperature and freezing
Keep kits within their labelled temperature range. Do not freeze. Discard any kit that has frozen, even if it has thawed.
Light and moisture
Keep kits in their original packaging until needed. Store them in a dry area away from direct light.
Shelf life
Check the expiry date on the individual pack before use. Discard expired kits.
Transport and receipt
Protect shipments from temperature extremes, moisture and physical damage. Inspect the packaging when stock arrives.
Manufacturing & Compliance

Manufacturing of the EXOSGFC system uses documented quality controls, regulatory procedures and lot tracking. Each production lot is tested and validated for consistent performance and patient safety.

ISO 13485:2016 Quality System
Production takes place in facilities certified to ISO 13485:2016, the international quality management standard for medical devices.
Validated Sterilization
Validated gamma irradiation is used for terminal sterilization, with records documenting sterility assurance.
CDSCO-Licensed Facility
Manufacturing is carried out in a medical device facility licensed by the Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation (CDSCO).
Batch Traceability
Records for each lot allow the finished device to be traced back to the raw materials used in production.
Quality Testing
Every production batch is tested for sterility and endotoxins and undergoes validation of functional performance.
Documentation Control
Complete device history records document component sourcing and process validation, as required by applicable regulations.

Regulatory Status. The EXOSGFC system is registered as a medical device with the relevant authorities. Marketing and distribution follow the applicable medical device rules in each jurisdiction where it is sold.

Important Disclaimer

EXOSGFC is a device for preparing autologous blood components. The device itself provides no therapy and has not been evaluated for diagnosing, treating, curing or preventing specific diseases.

Clinical outcomes
Results vary with patient health, underlying conditions, tissue quality and individual response. Technique, the clinical protocol and aftercare also affect outcomes.
Clinical responsibility
The qualified clinician is responsible for patient selection and decisions about dosing, administration and follow-up.
Training and informed consent
Use requires a licensed professional trained in blood-component handling and sterile technique. Discuss risks, limitations and realistic expectations with the patient when obtaining informed consent.
For Medical Professionals Only

EXOSGFC kits are intended for qualified, trained medical professionals.
