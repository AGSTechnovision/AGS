01 / The science

Autologous growth factors, exosome-based regeneration.

Osteogen is an advanced autologous orthobiologic solution designed to complement regenerative treatment protocols in orthopaedic and sports medicine practices.

Osteogen supports tissue repair, inflammation management, and musculoskeletal recovery through the body’s own regenerative potential.

What are autologous exosomes?

Autologous exosomes are naturally occurring nano-sized extracellular vesicles (30-150 nm) derived from a patient’s own cells. They act as biological messengers, carrying proteins, growth factors, lipids, mRNA, and microRNA between cells to regulate tissue repair and regeneration.
